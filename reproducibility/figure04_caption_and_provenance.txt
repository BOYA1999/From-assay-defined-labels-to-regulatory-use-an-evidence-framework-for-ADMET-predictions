Figure 4. Reported measurements across three case studies. (a,b) METTL3 P1 and P5 and their retrospective nearest training neighbours: mouse microsomal half-life and FRET IC50. (c) A2A synthetic intermediate 14 and generated target 13: human A2A Ki. (d–f) Industrial project compounds 2 and 5: species-specific microsomal half-lives, MDCK efflux ratio and target engagement assay. Point estimates are shown without confidence intervals, which were not supplied for these plotted comparisons. Comparator definitions differ; these are not tests of a common selector. The reported METTL3 half-life of 309 min is fitted beyond the 60-min sampling window. A2A PAMPA values are omitted because SI Table S8 does not state the unit. Numerical data are replotted from Matter and Caflisch, Figure 4 (10.1021/acsbiomedchemau.5c00198), Persico et al., Table 1 (10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c00231), and Rich et al., Table 1 (10.1021/acsmedchemlett.4c00290); all three main articles state CC BY 4.0.
